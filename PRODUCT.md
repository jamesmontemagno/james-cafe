# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AI Dev Day attendees in Tokyo, moving through a busy in-person event and using
their phones to join James Cafe's home-roasted coffee giveaway. The host also
needs a clear, theatrical control for drawing a winner in front of the crowd.

## Product Purpose

Turn a simple giveaway into a memorable shared moment. Attendees scan a QR code,
enter their name through a GitHub issue, watch their entry appear as a capsule,
and gather around for a live winner draw. Success means entry takes under a
minute, the machine feels alive as names arrive, and the final draw is legible
and delightful from across the room.

## Brand Personality

Joyful, mechanical, handcrafted. The voice mixes the tactile delight of a
Japanese capsule-toy machine with James Cafe's personal, small-batch hospitality
and AI Dev Day's energetic technology community.

## Anti-references

Not a generic conference landing page, casino raffle, corporate lead form,
childish arcade skin, or beige coffee-shop template. Avoid slot-machine
psychology, fake urgency, excessive glass effects, and decorative AI gradients.

## Design Principles

1. Make participation obvious at a glance, especially on a phone.
2. Turn every real attendee into a visible part of the machine.
3. Reward waiting with mechanical character rather than fake progress.
4. Treat English and Japanese as equal first-class experiences.
5. Keep the technology transparent: GitHub is the mechanism, not a mystery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with strong contrast, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful
status announcements, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives, and
layouts that remain usable at 200% zoom. Language can be switched at any time,
and the selected language is remembered.
